SmartFlood AI: An Intelligent Urban Flood Monitoring and Early Warning System
1. Problem
Urban flooding in Bangkok affects residents and businesses through property damage, traffic disruptions, and economic losses. Delayed warnings and limited access to reliable information can prevent people from preparing effectively. Although government agencies provide rainfall, water-level, and flood information, many residents may be unaware of these sources or rely mainly on news and social media. Technical data can also be difficult to interpret, limiting its usefulness for everyday decisions.
2. How I Would Use AI
SmartFlood AI would combine data analytics with generative AI to integrate weather conditions, rainfall, storm movement, drainage canal levels, and street flooding information from relevant government agencies.
Data analytics would assess flood conditions and determine warning levels using predefined criteria. Generative AI would then translate technical findings into clear explanations, answer location-specific questions, and provide practical recommendations based on verified information.
When recent or sufficient data is unavailable, the system would explain its limitations and direct users to official sources rather than generate unsupported answers. Users could access these services through a mobile application or website.
3. Impact
SmartFlood AI would provide a single platform for accessing and understanding flood information, reducing the time spent searching multiple sources. Unlike traditional approaches, users could ask questions in natural language and receive explanations relevant to their locations.
This could support faster travel planning, better preparedness, and more informed decisions, potentially reducing risks to personal safety and property.
4. Business Plan
The startup would begin with pilot projects in flood-prone districts such as Bang Kapi and Lat Krabang, offering free basic services to residents. After successful evaluation, it would expand across Bangkok.
Future development would include verified community reports, subscription-based flood risk forecasting for logistics companies and businesses, and collaboration with government agencies to improve public communication and emergency coordination.
